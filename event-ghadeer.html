Hadees Memorization Challenge!

Exclusively for boys up to 14 years old. Learn a Hadees, record your video, and get featured on our Instagram!

How to Participate?

1

Choose Your Hadees & Language

Click on the link provided on the website to view the selected Ahadees. Choose your preferred language:

English tap to view hadees → 👆 اردو حدیث دیکھنے کے لیے دبائیں → 👆 हिंदी हदीस देखने के लिए दबाएँ → 👆 ગુજરાતી હદીસ જોવા માટે દબાવો → 👆 Roman Urdu hadees dekhne ke liye dabayein → 👆

Audio Support: Audio support is available for all the traditions, we highly recommend listening to them carefully before recording to ensure perfect pronunciation and understanding!

2

Learn & Practice

Understand the meaning and memorize the Hadees and its translation in the language you selected.

3

Record Your Video (Important Instructions)

1 / 6
  • Video Framing: The kid should stand straight while recording and ensure they are clearly visible.
  • Stability: The phone should be held vertically (portrait mode) and must be kept steady/stable (not shaky) to ensure a high-quality video.
  • Clear Audio: Record in a quiet place with no background noise. Your voice must be loud and clear.
  • Accuracy: Make sure you recite both the Hadees and its translation correctly.

Recitation Protocol:

  • Start the recording by reciting:
    أَعُوذُ بِاللَّهِ مِنَ الشَّيْطَانِ الرَّجِيمِ بِسْمِ اللهِ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِيْمِ
  • End the recording by reciting the full Salawat:
    اللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ عَلَى مُحَمَّدٍ وَآلِ مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَجِّلْ فَرَجَهُمْ وَالْعَنْ أَعْدَاءَهُمْ
⚠️ NOTE: Agar aapne hadees ka arabic ya translation sahi se nahi padha to aapko wapas se record karke bhejne ke liye kaha jaega.
4

Send to Us

Once your video is ready, send it on WhatsApp: +91 8070 151 412

5

Get Featured

Our team will place your video into the special TSR GEMS template and upload it to our Instagram page.

6

Spread the Message

We'll share the link to your video. Share it on your Instagram Story, send to friends in DM, or tag friends in the comments. Winners are decided based on Likes.

⚠️ Important Rules & Guidelines
  • One Primary Entry: If a participant submits multiple videos (up to 5), only the one video with the most likes will be considered for the final selection.
  • Participation Prize: Please note that even if all 5 Hadiths are memorized, only one participation prize will be awarded per person.
  • No Reading / Cheating: Please ensure the Hadith is recited from memory. If we notice a participant is reading from a script, they will be asked to re-record and submit the video again properly.
  • Video Presentation & Privacy: To ensure the best quality and focus for your entry, the video frame should exclusively feature the participating child; please make sure no other individuals appear in the background. Additionally, the recording area must be clean, well-lit, and completely free of any inappropriate objects or distracting items that might take away from the sanctity of the activity. We highly recommend using a simple, uncluttered background to maintain a professional look and keep the audience's full attention on the recitation.
  • Review & Upload Process: Once a video is submitted, please allow up to 24 hours for our team to review the entry. During this time, we will verify the recitation for accuracy and ensure all guidelines are met. Once approved, the video will be templatized and uploaded to Instagram, and the official link will be shared with you for promotion.
