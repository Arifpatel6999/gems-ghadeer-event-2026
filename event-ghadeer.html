Hadees Memorization Challenge!

Exclusively for boys up to 14 years old. Learn a Hadees, record your video, and get featured on our Instagram!

How to Participate?

1

Choose Your Hadees & Language

Click on the link provided on the website to view the selected Ahadees. Choose your preferred language:

  • English
  • Urdu
  • Hindi
  • Gujarati
  • Roman Urdu

Audio Support: An audio file of the Hadees (Arabic with Urdu translation) is also available — listen carefully before recording!

2

Learn & Practice

Understand the meaning and memorize the Hadees and its translation in the language you selected.

3

Record Your Video (Important Instructions)

  • Video Framing: The kid should stand straight while recording and ensure they are clearly visible.
  • Stability: The phone should be held vertically (portrait mode) and must be kept steady/stable (not shaky) to ensure a high-quality video.
  • Clear Audio: Record in a quiet place with no background noise. Your voice must be loud and clear.
  • Accuracy: Make sure you recite both the Hadees and its translation correctly.

Recitation Protocol:

  • Start the recording by reciting:
    أَعُوذُ بِاللَّهِ مِنَ الشَّيْطَانِ الرَّجِيمِ بِسْمِ اللهِ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِيْمِ
  • End the recording by reciting the full Salawat:
    اللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ عَلَى مُحَمَّدٍ وَآلِ مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَجِّلْ فَرَجَهُمْ وَالْعَنْ أَعْدَاءَهُمْ
⚠️ NOTE: Agar aapne hadees ka arabic ya translation sahi se nahi padha to aapko wapas se record karke bhejne ke liye kaha jaega.
4

Send to Us

Once your video is ready, send it on WhatsApp: +91 8070 151 412

5

Get Featured

Our team will place your video into the special TSR GEMS template and upload it to our Instagram page.

6

Spread the Message

We'll share the link to your video. Share it on your Instagram Story, send to friends in DM, or tag friends in the comments. Winners are decided based on Likes.
