Ahadees — Eid-e-Ghadeer Challenge

Choose any one Hadees below to memorize and recite in your video.

1
قَالَ رَسُوْلُ اللهِ (ص): مَنْ آذَیْ عَلِیّاً فَقَدْ آذَانِیْ

The Messenger of Allah (s.a.w.a.) said:
"Whoever troubles Ali (a.s.) has indeed troubled me."

Behaar al-Anwaar, vol. 28, p. 410
2
قَالَ رَسُوْلُ اللهِ (ص): یَا عَلِیُّ حَاسِدُكَ حَاسِدِیْ وَ حَاسِدِیْ حَاسِدُ اللّٰهِ وَ حَاسِدُ اللّٰهِ فِی النَّارِ

The Messenger of Allah (s.a.w.a.) said:
"O Ali (a.s.), the one who envies you envies me; and the one who envies me envies Allah; and the one who envies Allah will be in the Fire."

Al-Amaali of Shaikh Tusi (a.r.), p. 624, Majlis 30, H. 1
3
قَالَ رَسُوْلُ اللهِ (ص): یَا عَلِیُّ سَعِدَ مَنْ تَوَلَّاكَ وَ شَقِیَ مَنْ عَادَاكَ

The Messenger of Allah (s.a.w.a.) said:
"O Ali (a.s.), fortunate is the one who loves you, and wretched is the one who opposes you."

Al-Amaali of Shaikh Saduq (a.r.), p. 24, Majlis 4, H. 8
4
قَالَ رَسُوْلُ اللهِ (ص): یَا عَلِیُّ شِیعَتُكَ شِیعَةُ اللّٰهِ وَ أَنْصَارُكَ أَنْصَارُ اللّٰهِ

The Messenger of Allah (s.a.w.a.) said:
"O Ali (a.s.), your followers are the followers of Allah, and your helpers are the helpers of Allah."

Al-Amaali of Shaikh Saduq (a.r.), p. 24, Majlis 4, H. 8
5
قَالَ رَسُوْلُ اللهِ (ص): یَا عَلِیُّ فَإِنَّ اللّٰهَ قَدْ بَاهَی بِكَ مَلَائِكَتَهُ

The Messenger of Allah (s.a.w.a.) said:
"O Ali (a.s.), indeed Allah takes pride in you before His angels."

Behaar al-Anwaar, vol. 38, p. 100, H. 19
